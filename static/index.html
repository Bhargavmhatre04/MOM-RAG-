FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EVERY DETAIL CLARIFIED.

How does NeuriaAI analyze meeting transcripts? +

NeuriaAI chunks uploaded PDF/TXT transcripts and direct notes, computes dense vector embeddings using SentenceTransformers, and indexes them in an ephemeral in-memory ChromaDB client. Queries and extraction requests run through domain-specific Google Gemini prompts grounded strictly in retrieved excerpts.

Which industries are supported out of the box? +

We provide 15 deep ontology profiles: IT & Software Development, Healthcare & Hospitals, Banking & Finance, Education & Universities, Manufacturing & Production, Construction & Real Estate, Retail & E-commerce, Human Resources, Marketing, Sales, Logistics & Supply Chain, Legal & Corporate, Government, Consulting, and General Business—plus a fully customizable Other Industry option.

Is my meeting data stored or persisted on disk? +

No. NeuriaAI utilizes an entirely in-memory ChromaDB vector client. Uploaded documents, chunk vectors, and generated reports exist solely in memory for the duration of the server session. Report downloads (PDF, TXT, CSV) are streamed directly from RAM BytesIO buffers.

How does NeuriaAI guarantee workspace isolation? +

Every workspace is partitioned with a unique UUID. All ChromaDB vector additions and similarity queries apply a strict metadata filter {"workspace_id": id}. A query in Workspace A cannot access, retrieve, or cite chunks stored in Workspace B.

Can I download audit-ready executive reports? +

Yes. You can stream branded in-memory PDF reports formatted with ReportLab, comprehensive raw TXT briefings, or CSV action item matrices with full task accountability fields (assignee, team, deadline, status, priority).

Does NeuriaAI invent action items or fabricate KPI numbers? +

Never. System prompts enforce strict anti-hallucination guardrails: if an assignee, deadline, or KPI metric is not explicitly stated in the source text, it is marked as 'Not specified'. If an answer is not in the text, RAG Ask replies exactly: "I couldn't find this information in the uploaded meeting documents."
